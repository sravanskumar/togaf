# Phase A — Architecture Vision

**Purpose:** Secure **agreement** to proceed with **this** architecture effort: stakeholders, concerns, and high-level direction.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Stakeholder** | Individual, team, or organization with **interest** in the architecture or its outcomes. |
| **Concern** | A **key interest** (e.g. cost, risk, compliance) the architecture should address for stakeholders. |
| **Architecture Vision** | A clear, communicable picture of **scope**, outcomes, and **target direction** at a high level. |
| **Business case / approval** | Justification and **decision** to invest in continuing the ADM cycle beyond the vision. |

## Functions (exam level)

- Identify **stakeholders** and **concerns**; bound the **initiative**.
- Describe **baseline** and **target** at a **high** level (not full B/C/D detail).
- Obtain **commitment** to continue into domain architecture work.

## Links to other phases

- **From Preliminary:** Uses **principles**, **governance**, and **EA capability** already in place.
- **To Phase B (and onward):** Vision and scope **constrain** business and IS/technology work.
- **Requirements Management:** Vision-level needs feed the ongoing requirements process.

## Boundaries

- **Not Preliminary:** Phase A is **per cycle / initiative**, not “stand up EA for the whole org.”
- **Not B/C/D:** Not the primary place for **detailed** business, data, application, or technology models.
