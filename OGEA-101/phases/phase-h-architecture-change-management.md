# Phase H — Architecture Change Management

**Purpose:** Keep the architecture **valid and current** — monitor **change**, capture new drivers, and **update** the architecture.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Change cycle / request** | Controlled way **new change** triggers reassessment and possible **re-entry** to ADM. |
| **Value realization** | Understanding whether delivered change achieves **intended** outcomes (exam recognition level). |
| **Architecture lifecycle** | The architecture as a **living** asset, not a one-off document. |

## Functions (exam level)

- Detect business and technology **changes** that affect the architecture.
- Initiate **updates** or new ADM iterations where appropriate.

## Links to other phases

- **From Phase G:** Operational reality and delivery feedback inform what must change.
- **Back to earlier phases:** May re-enter at **A**, **B**, **C**, etc., depending on the nature of change.
- **Requirements Management:** New and changed **requirements** from the environment feed the hub.

## Boundaries

- **Not Phase G:** H is **not** day-to-day **project conformance** policing — that is **G**.
- **Not Preliminary:** H does **not** replace standing **EA capability** setup (Preliminary).
