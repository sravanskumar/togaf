# Preliminary Phase

**Purpose:** Prepare the **organization** to do enterprise architecture — not the vision for a single initiative.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Scope** | Boundaries of EA work in the organization: what EA covers and what sits outside it for this context. |
| **Principles** | Architecture **decision rules** that constrain choices so trade-offs are resolved **consistently** with strategy. |
| **Frameworks** | Tailored **ADM** plus methods, templates, and structures so EA work is **repeatable**, not ad hoc. |
| **EA capability** | **People, roles, skills, and processes** needed to **run** EA (the function), not the architecture diagrams themselves. |
| **Governance basics** | **Who decides**, how **escalation** and **compliance** work for architecture so ADM outputs can be enforced. |

## Functions (exam level)

- Establish or improve **enterprise architecture capability** and **governance**.
- Agree **architecture principles** and **tailoring** of the method to the enterprise.
- Align the **scope** of EA with organizational context and strategy.

## Links to other phases

- **Enables all later phases:** Without capability and governance, cycles lack authority and consistency.
- **Requirements Management:** When ADM cycles run, requirements work still flows through the central hub.

## Boundaries

- **Not Phase A:** Preliminary does **not** produce the **Architecture Vision** or initiative-specific **business case** to proceed — that is Phase A.
- **Not B/C/D:** Does **not** develop domain architectures (business, IS, technology) in detail.
