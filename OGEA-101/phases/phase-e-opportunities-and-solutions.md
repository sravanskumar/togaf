# Phase E — Opportunities and Solutions

**Purpose:** Turn cross-domain gaps into **coherent change** — identify **solution directions** and **work packages**.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Work package** | A defined **chunk** of change (scope grouping) to plan, fund, or deliver. |
| **Consolidation** | Pulling together gaps and options from **B/C/D** into implementable **groupings**. |
| **Solution approach** | High-level way of **addressing** gaps (strategic options), before full migration timing detail. |

## Functions (exam level)

- Evaluate **strategic** options and major solution approaches.
- Group change into **work packages** and coherent initiatives.

## Links to other phases

- **From B/C/D:** Uses **domain** architectures and **gap** results.
- **To Phase F:** Work packages feed **migration planning**, sequencing, and roadmap.
- **Requirements Management:** Solution-level priorities and requirements update continuously.

## Boundaries

- **Not Phase F:** **Roadmap**, transition timing, and detailed **migration** planning are **F** (“**when** / how we move”), not E’s main label.
- **Not Phase G:** **Project-level conformance** and **contracts** are **G** (“**police during build**”).
