# Phase F — Migration Planning

**Purpose:** Plan **how and when** to move from baseline to target — **roadmap**, sequencing, **transition**.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Migration roadmap** | **Time-phased** plan of initiatives and dependencies. |
| **Sequencing** | **Order** of work packages and transitions to manage risk and dependencies. |
| **Transition Architecture** | Architectures describing **intermediate** states during the move (exam: recognize as migration-related). |

## Functions (exam level)

- Build an implementable **schedule** and path for change.
- Align programs, investments, and dependencies with the target architecture.

## Links to other phases

- **From Phase E:** **Work packages** and solution structure are sequenced here.
- **To Phase G:** The roadmap and constraints become what implementation must **conform** to.
- **Requirements Management:** Migration constraints and priorities feed the hub.

## Boundaries

- **Not Phase E:** E focuses on **what** major chunks and options; F focuses on **when** and **how** the transition unfolds.
- **Not Phase G:** F **plans** migration; G **governs** delivery against the architecture during projects.
