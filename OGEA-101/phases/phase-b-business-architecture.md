# Phase B — Business Architecture

**Purpose:** Develop **Business Architecture** — business **baseline** and **target** and the **gap** between them.

## Key terms

| Term | Short description |
|------|-------------------|
| **Business baseline** | The **current** business design relevant to the scope (capabilities, processes, organization, etc.). |
| **Business target** | The **desired** business design aligned to drivers and the Architecture Vision. |
| **Gap analysis** | Comparison of baseline vs target to find **missing or changed** capabilities and needs. |
| **View / viewpoint** | A **view** is a representation for stakeholders; a **viewpoint** defines the conventions for that class of views (exam: don’t swap them). |

## Functions (exam level)

- Describe how the business **operates** and should **operate** to meet change drivers.
- Produce business views that answer **business** stakeholder questions.
- Feed **information and application** needs into Phase C.

## Links to other phases

- **From Phase A:** Scoped by **Architecture Vision** and stakeholder agreement.
- **To Phase C:** Business outcomes drive **data** and **applications** architecture.
- **Requirements Management:** Business requirements are captured and refined continuously.

## Boundaries

- **Not Phase C:** Primary ownership of **Data** and **Applications** architectures is **C**, not B.
- **Not Phase D:** **Technology** platform architecture is developed in **D**.
